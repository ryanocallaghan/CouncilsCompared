Privacy

Councils compared doesn't use cookies, doesn't ask you to sign in, and doesn't keep anything that identifies you.

Who is responsible

I'm Ryan O'Callaghan. I run this website and I'm responsible for any personal data it handles. You can contact me through the contact form on my website.

Counting visits

Visits are counted with Umami, which runs on my own server, not an analytics or advertising company. It records the page you viewed, the website that sent you, your type of browser, device and operating system, and roughly where you are (country and town). It doesn't use cookies, doesn't record your IP address, and doesn't follow you across other websites. I only use the figures to see which pages are useful.

Searching by postcode

If you search by postcode, it is sent to postcodes.io, a free postcode lookup run by Ideal Postcodes, to find your council and neighbourhood. I don't store it, it isn't put in the page address, and it isn't recorded in the visit figures.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, and may keep it briefly in security logs.

If you get in touch

If you get in touch, I only use your message and contact details to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

The lawful basis for this processing is legitimate interests: running a website that people can use and that stays secure. You can ask me what data I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, through the contact form. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
